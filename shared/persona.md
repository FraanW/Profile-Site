# The Persona — Forward-Deployed Full-Stack Engineer

Both sites sell the same species of engineer, embodied by two different people. This file defines
the species. Person-specific truth lives in each site's `context/profile.md`.

## The one-line thesis

**An engineer you can drop into any problem — who ships an MVP that raises money, then scales it
properly once the money lands.**

"Forward-deployed" is the operative idea: not a ticket-taker waiting for specs, but someone who
lands in ambiguity (a founder's half-formed idea, a legacy mess, a market question), figures out
what actually needs to exist, and builds it end to end — product thinking, engineering, and
business outcome in one head.

## The two claims every page must prove

1. **Zero-to-one:** can turn a vague idea into a working, fundable product fast. Evidence looks
   like: MVPs shipped, funding raised on the back of them, business research and pitch decks that
   contributed to investor outcomes, speed with judgment.
2. **One-to-N:** can then scale that product like a real engineer, not an AI-slop merchant.
   Evidence looks like: production load handled, system design decisions, database/infra depth,
   testing discipline, code quality signals, publications, open-source standards work.

Most portfolios prove at most one of these. The entire point of these sites is proving both in
the same person. The tension between the two IS the positioning: "moves like a hacker, builds
like an engineer."

## On "AI-native" (and the slop paradox)

Both owners build *with* AI heavily and build AI products (agents, RAG, MCP). The sites must claim
this loudly — it's a differentiator, not a confession. But the claim only lands if the site itself
is proof of craft: AI-native means AI-accelerated engineering with human judgment and taste, the
opposite of prompt-and-pray codebases. Never let the copy or the design undermine that argument.

## Audiences (in priority order)

1. **Founders / early-stage teams** hiring a first engineer, a founding engineer, or an MVP
   builder. Read on mobile, from a shared link, in under a minute.
2. **Startup engineering managers** hiring full-stack / AI engineers. Read on desktop, will click
   GitHub links, allergic to inflated claims.
3. **Corporate hiring panels** screening candidates. Skim for signal: production experience,
   pedigree markers (IEEE, IIT, funded startups), stack match.

## Register

Confident builder. Talks about outcomes first, stack second. Never begs ("open to
opportunities!"), never influencer-postures, never corporate-speaks. The site is an artifact of
competence, not an application form.

## What this persona is NOT

- Not a "creative technologist" aesthetic playground
- Not a CV rendered in HTML
- Not a generalist shrug ("I do web, mobile, ML, design, and blockchain")
- Not a thought-leader blog wearing a portfolio's clothes
