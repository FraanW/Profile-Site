# Shama — Intake (blocking: no design or code until this becomes profile.md)

Shama's site follows the same persona (`shared/persona.md`) with their own truth and their own visual
identity. Build order rule from root `CLAUDE.md` applies: context first.

## What we need from Shama (or from Farhaan on their behalf, confirmed by them)

> Shama is non-binary: they/them in every document and every word of site copy, always.

**Identity**
- Full name as it should appear; location; email for the site; GitHub, LinkedIn, anything else live
- Any existing portfolio/site to replace

**The record (their master.md equivalent — the most important input)**
- Every role: employer, real title, dates, what they actually did (scope vs title, like Farhaan's
  EIR story), with numbers wherever they exist
- Projects with repos or live links; which they're proudest of and why
- Education, publications, certifications, awards
- Anything under NDA: what it is, and exactly what is publicly sayable

**Positioning**
- Their version of forward-deployed: which problems have they been dropped into?
- Zero-to-one evidence (shipped MVPs, business outcomes) and one-to-N evidence (scale,
  engineering depth) — the persona needs both
- Target reader priority: founders first or hiring managers first?

**Taste**
- 2–3 sites/brands whose feel they like (for Riker — direction input, not to copy)
- Photo on site: yes/no
- Domain preferences

## Process once received

1. Mimir + Lefler interview-style pass over the raw material → draft `context/profile.md`
2. Shama reviews and corrects → truth ledger locked
3. Then Riker opens their design direction (must not read as a reskin of Farhaan's site)
